使用說明

本工具於瀏覽器內以 Python（Pyodide＋numpy／scipy）進行空間插值，將作業場所定點濃度測量結果繪製為等值線分佈圖。所有運算皆在您的裝置完成，資料不會上傳。

操作步驟

  1. 等待運算環境載入：首次開啟需下載約 40 MB 運算環境（約 15–30 秒），之後由瀏覽器快取加速。右上角圓點轉為綠色即表示就緒。
  2. 設定場域尺寸：輸入作業場所的寬（X）與高（Y），單位公尺。分佈圖固定以此範圍與長寬比例繪製，座標原點 (0,0) 在左下角。
  3. 輸入測點資料：在表格直接輸入座標與濃度、點「上傳 CSV」匯入（自動辨識 x、y、c／concentration／濃度 欄位），或點「載入範例資料」試用。
  4. 資料品質檢核（QAQC）：系統自動排除 0 值、負值與超出場域範圍之測點（淡黃底標示）。疑似異常高值（大於 Q3＋3×IQR）會以 ⚠ 標記並預設納入計算；您可點擊該 ⚠ 圖示自行決定排除或重新納入，圖面與統計會即時更新。
  5. 檢視數理統計：圖表下方的統計框顯示樣本數、最小／最大值、算術平均、中位數、標準差、幾何平均（GM）、幾何標準差（GSD）與超過 OEL 之測點數，均以通過 QAQC（含您的異常值決定）之測點計算。
  6. 選擇插值方法與參數：五種方法各有適用情境（見下表）；可調整網格解析度、濃度單位與 OEL 閾值。
  7. 圖表設定：自訂標題、色階（點選色階圖示）、分佈圖不透明度、標註字體顏色與大小、濃度點大小。
  8. 平面圖（選用）：上傳 PNG／JPG 平面圖，預設自動縮放至場域大小；點「調整位置與大小」後，可直接在圖面上拖移藍框、拉動四角縮放，再點一次完成。若疊圖看不清楚，於「圖表設定」調低分佈圖不透明度即可讓底圖透出。
  9. 產生與匯出：點「產生分佈圖」繪製結果，再以「下載 PNG」或「下載網格 CSV」匯出。

介面操作小技巧

  • 調整輸入面板寬度：將游標移到左側面板右緣，出現 ↔ 後拖曳即可拉寬或拉窄。
  • 圖面縮放：在圖上滾動滑鼠滾輪即可平順縮放（以游標位置為中心）；按住拖曳可平移；雙擊圖面可回到完整場域範圍。

插值方法比較

方法優點限制建議使用情境
IDW
反距離加權		快速穩定；結果必落在實測值範圍內易出現「靶心」同心圓；無法估計高於／低於所有測點之值測點少（4–10 點）或需快速概覽

RBF
薄板樣條		曲面平滑、適合不規則散佈可能過衝（超出實測範圍）；點多時較慢10–100 點、濃度場預期平滑連續
Spline
雙三次樣條		對網格狀佈點可得很平滑曲面需先彙整為規則網格；少於 16 點自動改用 RBF系統性網格佈點且點數較多
Kriging
普通克利金		地統計最佳線性無偏估計，考慮空間自相關點少時半變異圖不可靠；計算量較大測點 ≥ 15 且具空間相關性、需正式估計

註：瀏覽器版 Pyodide 無法安裝 pykrige 時，工具自動改用內建純 Python 普通克利金（球型半變異模型），介面標示「Kriging（基礎實作 / basic implementation）」。

注意事項

  • 所有運算皆於瀏覽器內完成，測量資料與平面圖不會上傳。
  • 測點涵蓋範圍以外（凸包外）之估計屬外插，不確定性較高，解讀應謹慎。
  • 插值結果以 0 為下限（濃度不可能為負值）。
  • OEL 紅色超標區僅供視覺參考，不構成法規合規判定。詳見工具內「關於我們」之免責聲明。

User Guide

This tool performs spatial interpolation in your browser using Python (Pyodide + numpy/scipy) to turn point measurements into contour maps. All computation happens on your device; your data is never uploaded.

Step-by-step

  1. Wait for the runtime to load: the first visit downloads ~40 MB (15–30 s); later visits are cached. The dot in the top-right turns green when ready.
  2. Set the field size: enter the workplace width (X) and height (Y) in meters. The map always uses this extent and aspect ratio; the origin (0,0) is the bottom-left corner.
  3. Enter measurement data: type into the table, upload a CSV (columns x, y, c/concentration/濃度 are auto-detected), or load the sample dataset.
  4. Data QA/QC: zero values, negatives, and points outside the field are excluded automatically (pale-yellow rows). Suspected high outliers (above Q3 + 3×IQR) are flagged with ⚠ and included by default; click the ⚠ icon to exclude or re-include each one — the map and statistics update instantly.
  5. Review statistics: the box below the plot shows n, min/max, mean, median, SD, geometric mean (GM), geometric SD (GSD), and the count above the OEL, all computed on the QA/QC-passed points (reflecting your outlier choices).
  6. Choose a method and parameters: five interpolation methods (see table), grid resolution, unit, and an optional OEL threshold.
  7. Chart settings: title, color scale (click a swatch), contour opacity, label font color/size, and marker size.
  8. Floor plan (optional): upload a PNG/JPG plan — scaled to the field size by default. Click "Adjust position & size" to drag the blue box and pull its corners on the plot; click again to finish. If the overlay is unclear, lower the contour opacity under "Chart Settings".
  9. Generate & export: click "Generate Map", then download the PNG image or the interpolated grid CSV.

Interface tips

  • Resize the input panel: hover the right edge of the left panel and drag to make it wider or narrower.
  • Zoom the plot: scroll the mouse wheel over the plot for smooth, cursor-centered zoom; drag to pan; double-click to return to the full field extent.

Interpolation methods

MethodStrengthsLimitationsRecommended use
IDWFast, robust; results stay within the measured range"Bullseye" rings; cannot estimate beyond min/maxFew points (4–10) or quick overviews
LinearIntuitive; exact at points; no extrapolationOutside the hull filled with nearest values; facetedEven coverage; conservative presentation
RBFSmooth surface; good for scattered pointsMay overshoot the measured range; slower at scale10–100 points; smooth continuous field
SplineVery smooth over grid-like layoutsBins to a regular grid; <16 points falls back to RBFSystematic grid sampling with more points
KrigingGeostatistical BLUE; accounts for spatial correlationUnreliable variogram with few points; heavier compute≥15 spatially-correlated points; formal estimates

Note: when pykrige cannot be installed under Pyodide, the tool falls back to a built-in pure-Python Ordinary Kriging (spherical variogram), labeled "Kriging (basic implementation)".

Notes

  • All computation runs in the browser; measurement data and floor plans are never uploaded.
  • Estimates outside the convex hull are extrapolations with higher uncertainty — interpret with caution.
  • Interpolated values are floored at 0 (concentrations cannot be negative).
  • The red OEL exceedance zone is a visual aid only, not a compliance determination. See the full disclaimer under "About" in the tool.
