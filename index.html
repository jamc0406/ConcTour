關於我們

本工具用於將作業場所危害性化學品之定點測量結果，繪製為濃度空間分佈圖（等值線圖），協助職業衛生人員辨識高暴露熱區、規劃採樣與改善通風配置，並作為風險溝通之輔助工具。

隱私與資料安全：所有運算（含插值與統計）皆透過 WebAssembly 技術（Pyodide＋numpy／scipy）於您的瀏覽器內完成；輸入之測量資料與平面圖不會上傳至任何伺服器，本工具亦不蒐集、儲存任何資料。

技術架構：Pyodide（瀏覽器內 Python）、numpy／scipy（數值運算與插值）、Plotly.js（圖表渲染）。原始碼為單一 HTML 檔案，可部署於任何靜態網頁空間。

About

This tool turns point measurements of hazardous chemical concentrations in workplaces into spatial contour maps, helping occupational hygienists identify exposure hot spots, plan sampling, evaluate ventilation layouts, and communicate risks.

Privacy & data safety: all computation (interpolation and statistics) runs inside your browser via WebAssembly (Pyodide + numpy/scipy). Your measurement data and floor plans are never uploaded to any server, and the tool collects and stores nothing.

Technology: Pyodide (Python in the browser), numpy/scipy (numerical computing and interpolation), and Plotly.js (rendering). The source is a single HTML file deployable on any static hosting.

Version v1.2.0 (June 2026). See the "User Guide" page for detailed instructions. For development and contact information, please reach the site administrator.
